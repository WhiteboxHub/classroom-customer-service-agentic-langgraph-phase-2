# XYZ Corp Benefits Policy 2025

## 1. Dental Coverage
XYZ Corp provides comprehensive dental coverage for all eligible employees.
- **Preventive Services**: Covered at **100%**. This includes bi-annual cleanings, exams, and X-rays.
- **Major Services**: Covered at **80%**. This includes root canals, crowns, and bridges.
- **Orthodontics**: Covered at **50%** up to a lifetime maximum of $2,000.

## 2. Out-of-Network Rules
Services received from providers outside the XYZ Corp network are subject to different coverage rates.
- **Co-insurance**: You are responsible for **50%** of the allowed amount for any out-of-network service.
- **Deductible**: Out-of-network services have a separate $1,000 deductible per individual.
- **Balance Billing**: You may be responsible for the difference between the provider's charge and the allowed amount.

## 3. Appeal Process
If a claim is denied, members have the right to appeal.
- **Filing Deadline**: Appeals must be filed within **30 days** of receiving the Explanation of Benefits (EOB).
- **Submission**: Appeals can be submitted online via the member portal or by mail to the Claims Department.
- **Review Timeline**: XYZ Corp will review appeals and provide a decision within 60 days.
